# Locke brand mark

**The L holds the coin.** A single L (Locke) wraps around a coin, the asset a business holds.
Protection around what you own.

## Construction

- 4-unit grid on a 48-unit square. The L's stroke is 16 units (one module).
- The coin sits in the open quarter with an equal 3.8-unit gap to both arms. It is slightly
  larger than the quarter, so it reads as the same weight as the L.
- Outer corners of the L are rounded at 3.5 units; inner corners stay crisp.

## Wordmark

Inter Tight Medium, −3% tracking. The mark is 1.32× the wordmark's cap height and sits
0.42 cap heights from it, centred on the cap height.

## Colour

| Paper | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, reverse L |

## Rules

- Clear space: one stroke width (16 units, a quarter of the mark) on every side.
- Minimum size: mark 16 px, lockup 80 px wide.
- Don't recolour the L and the coin the same colour unless the whole mark is one colour.
- Don't rotate, outline, add effects, or move the coin out of the L's corner.
- On cobalt, use the paper L with an ink coin. On lime, use the ink L with a cobalt coin.
